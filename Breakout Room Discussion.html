Main Discussion Questions

1. When you hear news about animals close to extinction, how does it make you feel?
It makes me feel sad and frustrated. It reminds me that humans should protect animals and nature more.
2. Do you think small actions by individuals can make a difference for environmental problems?
Yes, I think small actions matter. If many people recycle, reduce plastic, and protect nature, it can create a big change.
3. Have you ever changed your behavior because of environmental concerns?
Yes, I try to reduce plastic use and recycle more. I also try to support eco-friendly products.
4. What do you think is the biggest challenge in protecting endangered animals?
The biggest challenge is balancing human needs with nature. Many industries destroy habitats to make money.
5. If you could focus on protecting one endangered species, which would you choose and why?
I would protect sea turtles because they are important for the ocean ecosystem and they are often affected by pollution.

🐾 Hypothetical & Fun Questions

Discuss these creative questions with your group.

1. If animals could talk, what would they say about humans?
They might say humans are destroying nature and should protect the planet.
2. If you could become any animal for one day, which would you choose?
I would become a bird so I could fly and see the world from the sky.
3. If one animal became the smartest species on Earth, which would it be?
Maybe dolphins, because they are already very intelligent.
4. If animals had their own government, which animal would be the leader?
Elephants might be good leaders because they live in strong family groups.
5. If you could design the perfect habitat for an endangered animal, what would it look like?
It would be a large protected forest with clean water and no hunters.
6. If humans disappeared tomorrow, what would happen to endangered animals?
Many animals might recover because their habitats would be protected.
7. If you could invent a machine to protect animals, what would it do?
It could detect illegal hunters and alert wildlife protection teams.
8. If you could bring back one extinct animal, which would it be?
I would bring back the woolly mammoth because it is a fascinating ancient species.
9. If animals could vote, which environmental rule would they support first?
They would probably vote to stop deforestation and protect forests.
10. If animals could send a message to humans, what would it say?
Please protect the planet because it is our home too.
