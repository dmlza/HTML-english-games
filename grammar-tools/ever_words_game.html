🌟 Whatever Words Game

🎯 ELI5: "EVER" Words = CHOICE Words!
Simple Rule: These words mean "ANY + something" or "it doesn't matter which one"
Teaching Tip: Use gestures! Point everywhere for "wherever", shrug shoulders for "whatever"
WHATEVER
Any THING (I don't care which)
"Eat whatever you want" = "Eat any food"
WHENEVER
Any TIME (I don't care when)
"Come whenever you can" = "Come any time"
WHEREVER
Any PLACE (I don't care where)
"Sit wherever you like" = "Sit any place"
WHOEVER
Any PERSON (I don't care who)
"Whoever arrives first wins" = "Any person who..."
HOWEVER
BUT (contrast) / Any WAY
"It's expensive. However, I'll buy it" = "BUT I'll buy it"
WHICHEVER
Any CHOICE between options
"Choose whichever looks better" = "Choose any option"
⚠️ Common Mistakes:
❌ "I go whatever place" → ✅ "I go wherever I want"
❌ "However person comes" → ✅ "Whoever comes first"
❌ "I like whichever food" → ✅ "I like whatever food you cook"
