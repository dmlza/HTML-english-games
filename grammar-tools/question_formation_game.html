❓ Question Formation Game

📖 How to Make Questions - Super Simple!

1. Habits & Facts (things you do often)
"I like pizza" → "Do I like pizza?"
"She likes pizza" → "Does she like pizza?"
Look for: I/you/we/they = DO | he/she/it = DOES
2. Past Actions (yesterday, last week, this morning)
"I played soccer yesterday" → "Did I play soccer yesterday?"
"She went home last night" → "Did she go home last night?"
Look for: Time words like yesterday, last night, this morning = always DID
3. AM/IS/ARE/WAS/WERE - Just move to front!
"She is happy" → "Is she happy?"
"They were late yesterday" → "Were they late yesterday?"
Look for: am/is/are/was/were in the sentence = move it to the front!
4. CAN/WILL/SHOULD - Just move to front!
"I can swim" → "Can I swim?"
"She will come tomorrow" → "Will she come tomorrow?"
Look for: can/will/should in the sentence = move it to the front!
