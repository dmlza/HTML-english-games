📚 Parts of Speech

📘
Noun — person, place, thing.
dog, park, teacher, city, book, ocean, friend, school
🃏
Verb — action/state.
run, is, jump, think, sleep, sing, laugh, create
🎨
Adjective — describes noun.
big, happy, red, scary, funny, ancient, brave, tiny
⚡
Adverb — modifies verb.
quickly, well, carefully, loudly, never, always, here
🙋
Pronoun — replaces nouns.
he, it, she, they, we, you, this, that
➕
Conjunction — joins ideas.
and, because, but, or, so, when, if, although
📍
Preposition — relation.
in, at, on, under, over, through, beside, during
🅰️
Article — a, an, the.
Intro checklist
Sentence Frames:
  • One day, [subject] [verb] [object].
  • A long time ago, [subject] [verb] in [place].
  • Early that morning, [subject] was [verb+ing] [object].
Climax checklist
Sentence Frames:
  • Suddenly, [subject] [verb] [details].
  • Out of nowhere, [subject] [verb] [object].
  • Without warning, [subject] [verb] because [reason].
Ending checklist
Sentence Frames:
  • Finally, [subject] [verb] [result].
  • In the end, [subject] learned that [lesson].
  • After that, [subject] [verb] and [result].
