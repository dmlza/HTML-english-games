PAST
What happened?
(Answer in past tense)
Practice
"Earlier, she ______"
"As I remember, he ______"
Examples
"Earlier, she walked."
"As I remember he was buying pizza"
PRESENT
What is happening?
(Describe what you see now)
Practice
"Right now, she is ______"
"At this moment, he is ______"
Examples
she is running
looking at the pig
sitting down
FUTURE
What's going to happen?
(Answer in future tense)
Practice
"Next, she will ______"
"I think he will ______"
Examples
escape
find something
get caught
eat pizza
The Concept
What do we call this action?
(Usually ends in -tion, -ing, -ment)
Practice
"The ______" / "An ______" / "A ______"
Examples
the running, an escape, a transformation
Manner
How are they doing it?
(Use adverbs - words ending in -ly or describing HOW)
Practice
"She is ______ running"
"He ______ opened the door"
Examples
frantically, quietly, desperately, slowly, carefully
Inner Feelings vs Outer Appearance
Inner feelings: What does the character feel inside?
Practice
"She feels ______" / "He is feeling ______"
Outer appearance: What does the character show outside?
Practice
"She looks ______" / "He appears ______"
Examples
nervous, calm, angry, tired, excited

Most Common

Happy
When you smile and feel good inside
Sad
When you want to cry or feel down
Angry
When you're mad and want to yell
Scared
Your heart beats fast, you want to hide
Surprised
Something unexpected happened
Worried
You think something bad will happen
Confused
When you don't understand what's happening
Excited
When you can't wait for something good
Tired
When you want to sleep or rest
Bored
When nothing seems fun or interesting

Less Common

Disgusted
Makes you say "eww, gross!"
Proud
When you feel good about something you did
Embarrassed
When your face gets red from awkwardness
Jealous
When you want what someone else has
Guilty
When you feel bad about doing something wrong
Curious
When you really want to know more
Relieved
When something scary or worrying stops
Frustrated
When you're stuck and can't do what you want
Suspicious
When you think someone is hiding something
Amazed
When something is so cool it blows your mind
Annoyed
When something bothers you and makes you a little angry
