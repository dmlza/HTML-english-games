Reading

Many people are now holding onto their smartphones longer than before. This is partly because phones are becoming more expensive. Also, updates are not as exciting as they used to be.

Experts say smartphones can last up to five years if users take good care of them. Technology expert Chris Hauk says that with proper care and updates, most phones can give four or five years of use.

The battery is one of the most important parts affecting smartphone life. Apple explains that lithium-ion batteries slowly lose their ability to hold charge over time.

Samsung advises keeping the battery above 50 percent and avoiding letting it drop to zero. Repeatedly draining the battery completely can shorten its lifespan.

Extreme temperatures can also damage smartphones. Heat above 35°C can permanently reduce battery life.

Changing phone settings can help save power. Lower brightness, dark mode, and shorter screen timeouts can help.

Experts also recommend protecting phones with strong cases and keeping them clean. Finally, installing system updates regularly helps security and performance.

Vocabulary Activity

Instruction: In what situation will we be able to use these words?

1. hold onto

Meaning: keep something

I plan to hold onto my laptop for five years.
Talking about keeping something instead of replacing it.
2. updates

Meaning: new software improvements

My phone needs a system update.
Talking about improving apps or software.
3. lifespan

Meaning: how long something lasts

Exercise increases lifespan.
Talking about the life length of objects or people.
4. rechargeable battery

Meaning: battery that can be charged again

My headphones use a rechargeable battery.
Talking about electronic devices.
5. diminish

Meaning: become smaller

Battery power diminishes over time.
Talking about something slowly decreasing.
6. peak performance

Meaning: best possible performance

Athletes train for peak performance.
Talking about the best condition of something.
7. charging history

Meaning: record of how a battery was charged

Charging history affects battery life.
Talking about battery usage patterns.
8. extreme temperatures

Meaning: very hot or very cold weather

Extreme temperatures damage electronics.
Talking about weather affecting devices.
9. power settings

Meaning: phone settings controlling energy use

I changed my power settings to save battery.
Talking about saving battery life.
10. screen brightness

Meaning: how bright the screen is

Lower screen brightness saves power.
Talking about adjusting phone display.
