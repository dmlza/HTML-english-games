Vocabulary 1

Read the words.

  • climate change – long-term changes in Earth’s temperature
  • endangered – animals that may disappear in the future
  • extinct – animals that no longer exist
  • habitats are being destroyed – animals are losing their natural homes
  • hunted for their fur / horns / shells / teeth – animals killed for body parts
  • pollution – harmful substances in air, water, or land
  • elephant
  • gorilla
  • orangutan
  • panda
  • rhinoceros
  • tiger

Vocabulary 2

1. These African wild dogs are [extinct / polluted / hunters / endangered]
Correct: endangered extinct – animals that no longer exist polluted – contaminated with harmful substances hunters – people or animals that hunt endangered – animals that may disappear in the future
2. [Elephants / Pandas / Tigers / Rhinoceros] are also in trouble. There are fewer than 1,600 living in the wild.
Correct: Pandas Elephants – large mammals with trunks Pandas – black-and-white bears that are endangered Tigers – large striped cats Rhinoceros – large animals with horns
3. They face many problems because their [hunters / hunts / habitats / humans] are being destroyed.
Correct: habitats hunters – people who hunt animals hunts – the act of hunting habitats – the natural home where animals live humans – people
4. [Pollution / Habitats / Extinction / Populations] can harm animals that live on land, in water, or in the air.
Correct: Pollution Pollution – harmful substances in the environment Habitats – places where animals live Extinction – when a species disappears forever Populations – number of animals in a species
5. The black [elephant / gorilla / orangutan / rhinoceros] is often hunted for its horns.
Correct: rhinoceros elephant – large animal with a trunk gorilla – large ape orangutan – ape that lives in forests rhinoceros – animal hunted for its horn
6. You’d probably have to visit a zoo to see one of these [orangutans / elephants / tigers / pandas].
Correct: pandas orangutans – large forest apes elephants – large mammals with trunks tigers – striped big cats pandas – endangered bears rarely seen in the wild

Vocabulary 3

1. These turtles are in danger because they are [endangered / extinct / hunted / polluted] for their shells.
Correct: hunted endangered – at risk of extinction extinct – no longer exists hunted – killed by people for body parts polluted – affected by environmental contamination
2. There are so few of these giant cats alive that they are nearly [populated / polluted / extinct / endangered].
Correct: extinct populated – having many individuals polluted – contaminated environment extinct – no animals left endangered – very close to extinction
3. This [tiger / gorilla / panda / rhinoceros] may die out because many people hunt it.
Correct: tiger tiger – large striped cat hunted illegally gorilla – large forest ape panda – black-and-white bear rhinoceros – horned animal
4. Polar bears are affected by [climate / pollution / population / extinction] change.
Correct: climate climate – long-term weather patterns pollution – harmful substances population – number of individuals extinction – disappearance of a species
5. The mountain [panda / gorilla / orangutan / tiger] population is increasing.
Correct: gorilla panda – bamboo-eating bear gorilla – large mountain ape orangutan – forest ape tiger – large striped cat
6. Half the wild population of this [rhinoceros / tiger / orangutan / elephant] has been lost in one generation.
Correct: elephant rhinoceros – horned mammal tiger – large cat orangutan – forest ape elephant – large mammal with trunk
